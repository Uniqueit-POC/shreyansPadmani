Considering the client request, we decided to promote a Proof-of-Concept for Intellectual Document Processing solution. The solution itself is based on the principle of intelligent document analysis, which is further connected with automated data processing.

Before we get to the detailed presentation of the solution we presented, let’s explore the two core principles that drive this architecture.

What Is Intelligent Document Processing ?

Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) is a term that describes the usage of advanced technologies such as AI, ML, and NLP.

All these technologies are used to capture, extract, and process document information, turning unstructured content into high-quality, actionable data.

Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning NLP

Structured Data Capture

Visualizing the transition from sample doc source to structured data.

For business purposes IDP allows, for instance, to extract relevant data, such as dates, names, numbers and amounts from the files, scans or photos sent by the clients. The processing itself provides all the necessary information for business, making it convenient for further analysis.

The main tool for that is optical character recognition (OCR) mechanism and this mechanism requires additional overview.

When you receive a scan, photo or PDF, the OCR program finds the coordinates of the text parts which should be processed. It also defines whether the text is presented in the form of table, formula, etc. When all the characters or symbols are analyzed, the IDP identifies their semantics.

The main benefit of using such a form of document analysis is that the program does the entire work for you. Instead of dealing with a team of employees who will read through everything that the company receives, it provides automated data extraction from the document, highlighting the key points and making them ready for further analysis.

What Is Automated Data Processing?

IDP, however, is only the first step to decision-making optimization. It goes on with the second principle: Automated Data Processing (ADP). This is based on the usage of technologies, including AI, which analyze structured data to get necessary information.

For instance, through ADP, businesses can get insights or detailed reports and spreadsheets that help make decision-making faster and easier. As an example, we’ve developed solutions referring to AI predictive analytics that allowed our clients to decide which banner would be the most attractive for their website visitors.

In our case, the bank had the opportunity to see whether a client is reliable for credit or not. They could instantly access information regarding credit history, incomes, and obligatory payments, such as taxes and utility bills.

Our Solution

Getting acquainted with the client's needs, we offered a solution that helps get rid of unnecessary work connected with time-consuming document analysis.

★ Proven expertise from our previous AI resume screening tools.

01

Eliminating the "Painstaking" Data Gathering

Typically, AI extraction requires gathering and analyzing hundreds of samples to train the system—a slow process with no immediate results. We spared our client from this bottleneck.

By identifying key value types from a small set of popular documents, we optimized the initial work from weeks to just a few seconds. We applied the same high-efficiency logic used in our previous contract analysis solutions.

02

OCR-Powered Classification & Extraction

Once the foundation was set, we deployed AI document processing based on OCR technology. This allows the bank to efficiently process the massive volume of documents they receive daily.

The software now serves as the vital link between unstructured client documents and productive automated analysis, covering both document classification and precise data extraction.
