Vocabulary and Spelling App

To enhance the legacy “Buzzy Bee Spelling App” by building a comprehensive cross-platform educational app with advanced features like AI-generated word lists, gamified learning, teacher-parent-student role systems, and adaptive learning paths.

AI in EdTech Vocabulary Learning Spelling Games Adaptive Learning Multi-role App Educational Gamification

Project Objectives

A cross-platform, gamified vocabulary learning app with AI integration and multi-role support.

Create a fully functional, engaging, and scalable Vocabulary and Spelling App that:

  • Supports schools, teachers, parents, and students

  • Enables role-based content creation, scheduling, and management

  • Integrates modern features such as AI list generation and collaborative learning

  • Is accessible across web, Android, iOS, and tablet platforms

Problem Statement & Challenges

Build a modern educational app combining AI, gamification, and cross-device synchronization.

  • Existing spelling apps lacked modern features and AI-generated content capabilities

  • No structured way for teachers and parents to control, schedule, or customize content

  • Limited gamification elements, progress tracking, and adaptive learning paths for different student levels

  • Required real-time device synchronization with fallback support for offline access

Project Approach / Solution

Designed a gamified, AI-enabled learning platform tailored for schools, teachers, and families.

Designed modular, scalable app structure using BRD and Mind Maps

Built role-based access system with content locking and release logic

Integrated AI tools (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini) for smart word list generation

Gamified app via avatars, digital currency, leaderboards, and quizzes

Developed wireframes for mobile and admin portals with intuitive UX

Key Benefits

Cross-platform availability

Accessible via Web, iOS, Android, and Fire Tablets

AI-powered content creation

Teachers and parents can generate smart spelling lists using OpenAI or Google Gemini

Engaging learning experience

Includes avatars, badges, treasure chests, flashcards, and multiple interactive games

Personalized learning paths

Adaptive quizzes and vocabulary lists based on each student’s proficiency and progress

Parental and teacher control

Role-based permissions for creating, scheduling, and managing word lists and quizzes

Collaborative learning

Supports class-level assignments, shared lists, and multiplayer spelling challenges

Detailed progress tracking

Tracks missed words, milestone achievements, and overall improvement over time

Gamification with purpose

Level-ups, digital rewards, leaderboards, and streaks drive motivation and retention

Offline learning support

Selected features work without internet, ensuring uninterrupted access

Flexible pricing and access

Family accounts, 30-day promo codes, and a 7-day free trial with subscription option

Result

Delivered a ready-to-develop blueprint for a modern, AI-powered vocabulary learning app through rich documentation, engaging wireframes, and gamified UX strategy—poised for development success and future scaling.
